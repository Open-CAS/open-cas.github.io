Open CAS is developed and maintained by [Unvertical](https://unvertical.com/),
which offers commercial support for organizations running Open CAS in
production.

## What we offer

* **Deployment and tuning** — sizing the cache, choosing cache modes, cleaning
  and promotion policies for your workload, and validating the result.
* **Signed and validated packages** — ready-to-install, signed Open CAS Linux
  packages, validated on the major Linux distributions.
* **Long-term maintenance** — builds and fixes for specific kernel versions and
  Open CAS releases beyond what the upstream project maintains.
* **Integration** — bringing OCF into your own storage stack, or adapting
  Open CAS Linux and the SPDK block device to your platform.
* **Custom development** — new features, performance work and changes carried
  upstream so they stay maintained.
* **SLA-backed support** — direct access to the maintainers and agreed response
  times.
* **Training** — sessions on Open CAS architecture, operation and debugging for
  your engineering and operations teams.

## Getting in touch

Use the [contact form at unvertical.com](https://unvertical.com/#contact) with a
short description of your setup and what you need, and we will get back to you.

## Community support

Open CAS is free and open source software, and questions and defect reports are
always welcome on GitHub at no cost. See [Contributing](/contributing/) for how
issues are tracked and what to include in a report.
